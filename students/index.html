W2W+ Students: privacy policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

W2W+ Students is a Chrome extension that shows a student-worker WhenToWork schedule in easier-to-use views. It is not made by or affiliated with WhenToWork or Google.

What it reads

  • Your schedule data from your own WhenToWork account (staff names, shifts, positions, shift categories and notes), read through the WhenToWork API using the API key you enter.
  • WhenToWork's own Trade Board and Shift History pages, using the sign-in of the WhenToWork tab you have open, to show which shifts were dropped and a shift's history.
  • Whether the WhenToWork page you have open is signed in, so it knows when to show its button.

The Chat panel

  • The Chat panel shows a separate Google Apps Script web page (a viewer for a Google Chat space) inside the extension. That page is run by Google and only opens for people the page's owner has given access; everyone else sees Google's sign-in or "no access" screen.
  • The extension tells that page which day, location or person you are looking at, so it can follow along. It does not copy, save or send on any chat messages. Google's own privacy policy covers that page.

What it saves, and where

  • Your WhenToWork API key, your settings (location, search terms, theme) and the proposed changes you make in it.
  • All of this is saved only in your own Chrome browser, on your computer. Removing the extension deletes it.

Where data goes

  • The extension connects only to WhenToWork (whentowork.com), plus the Google page in the Chat panel described above.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else. There are no analytics, ads or tracking.
  • No data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties, and it is not used for anything other than showing your schedule.
  • The extension only reads. It never changes anything in WhenToWork.

Questions

Use the contact email on the extension's Chrome Web Store page.
